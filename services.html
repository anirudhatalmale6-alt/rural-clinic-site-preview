What we care for.

Rural general practice covers a lot of ground. Wording below is draft copy for you to approve or replace.

01 General practiceEveryday medical care for all ages — acute illness, injuries and ongoing concerns. → 02 Women’s healthCervical screening, contraception, family planning, menopause and general women’s health. → 03 Children’s healthChildhood illness, development and growth checks, and immunisations. → 04 Chronic disease managementStructured ongoing care and care plans, coordinated with your other providers. → 05 Preventative healthHealth checks, screening and risk assessment aimed at finding things early. → 06 Skin checks & proceduresSkin examinations and minor in-clinic procedures. → 07 Occupational medicalsPre-employment, industry and licence-related medicals and assessments. → 08 Health assessmentsMedicare health assessments for eligible patients across age groups. → 09 Mental healthAssessment, mental health care plans, review and referral to further support. → 10 ImmunisationsRoutine and travel-related vaccinations for children and adults. →

Adding a service later

Each service is one entry with a title, a short description and an optional detail page. Rail medicals, aviation medicals and other assessments slot straight in as new entries — the list simply grows, the design doesn’t change and no developer is needed.

Nothing is listed here until you confirm the practice actually offers it.
