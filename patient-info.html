What to bring

Medicare card, any concession or DVA card, private health details if relevant, a list of your current medicines, and any letters, results or paperwork you have been given.

Repeat prescriptions

Repeat prescriptions can be requested online using the repeat prescription request form. Requests are not automatically approved — every request is reviewed by a clinician and may require an appointment.

Repeat referrals

Repeat referrals to a specialist you already see can be requested online. Referrals for a new problem, or to a specialist you have not seen before, need an appointment.

Results and recalls

Results are not given out over the phone unless the practice has told you otherwise. If your doctor needs to discuss a result with you, the practice will contact you to arrange an appointment.

Medical certificates require an appointment. A certificate cannot be backdated or issued without an assessment.
