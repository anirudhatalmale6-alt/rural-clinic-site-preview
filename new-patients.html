New patients

Welcome to the practice.

Registering before you arrive means your first appointment is spent with the doctor, not at the front desk.

Register as a new patient Book your first appointment

Confirm whether the practice is accepting new patients, and any conditions

1

Register online

Complete the registration form on your phone or computer — your details, history, medicines, allergies and Medicare information.

2

Book your appointment

Choose a time and a doctor online, or phone the practice. Longer appointments can be requested if you have several things to discuss.

3

Come and see us

Bring your Medicare card, any concession or DVA card, your medicines list and any letters or results you have.

Before you arrive

What to bring.

  • Medicare card, and any concession, pension or DVA card
  • Private health fund details, if relevant
  • A list of your current medicines, including anything from the chemist
  • Letters, results, imaging or paperwork you have been given
  • Anything else the practice wants new patients to bring

Whether previous medical records are transferred, and how that is requested — to be supplied

Your information

Registration details go directly into the practice’s own clinical system over a secure connection. They are not stored on this website, and this website never holds your medical record.
